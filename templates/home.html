Community Heatwave Protection Network

A direct municipal-to-neighbourhood dispatch system connecting vulnerable residents, field volunteers, and ward administrators during severe heatwaves across India.

The Problem

Public Health Risk

During severe Indian summer heatwaves, elderly residents living alone, outdoor manual labourers, and families living under uninsulated tin or asbestos roofs face life-threatening heatstroke without anyone knowing their immediate condition.

Dense urban settlements turn unventilated rooms into extreme heat traps where daytime indoor temperatures routinely exceed outdoor ambient air.

Traditional municipal advisories remain passive public broadcasts that fail to identify exactly which vulnerable households require urgent welfare visits, oral rehydration salts, or emergency medical evacuation today.

How HeatShield Works

Three coordinated operational steps executed daily in every monitored ward.

Step 1

Triage & Risk Scoring

Every registered resident is scored by physiological and housing vulnerability factors: age 60+, living alone, tin or asbestos roofing, outdoor physical work, and chronic health conditions.

Step 2

Single-Tap Check-ins & SOS

Residents tap "I'm okay" each morning. If an unattended senior misses check-in by the 12:00 PM cutoff on a Red heat day or triggers SOS, they are instantly flagged at the top of the visit queue.

Step 3

Dispatched Field Visits

Ward volunteers receive mobile-optimized visit routes sorted by vulnerability and distance to deliver ORS packets, cold drinking water, and coordinate immediate cooling centre evacuation.
